Find a place on map searches geographic locations independently of the corrosion database. It can locate cities, regions, countries, and other mapped places even when no corrosion site is recorded there. Search dataset filters only corrosion records already contained in this database, including site label, site ID, site type, country/location, administering country, former entity, region category, exposure period, metal, and notes.
